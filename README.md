The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line — the single sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting.

## The entry

> The meeting is over: we agreed to have another meeting to decide what we decided in this meeting.

97 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **One sentence that replaces the meeting** — the meeting's own verdict on itself, unmistakably office humor.
2. **Kills the meeting outright** — after this line, no one could justify holding the meeting at all: it produced only an agreement to meet again about what it decided.
3. **Relatable** — drawn from real office life; anyone who has sat through a useless meeting has watched one decide nothing.
4. **Lands as a joke** — the recursion is the sting, and the turn arrives in the last words.
5. **Stands alone** — one line, no context, no explanation, no setup.
